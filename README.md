## Sources

- `calm-in-the-storm`: A guided prayer to quiet the body and soul when stress feels overwhelming, grounded in Psalm 23, Philippians 4:6-7, and Psalm 131:2.
- `catholic-guided-prayer`: Sign of the Cross, Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be, brief examen, and intercession.
- `confess-and-be-forgiven`: A guided prayer of honest confession, receiving God's pardon and forgiveness, grounded in 1 John 1:9 and Psalm 32:5.
- `create-in-me-a-clean-heart`: A guided prayer for the removal of hidden sins and the renewal of the inner life, rooted in Psalm 51:10 — asking God to create a clean heart and a steadfast spirit.
- `faith-in-gods-faithfulness`: A guided meditation on Habakkuk 2:4 — the just live by faith — with verses that encourage trust in God's steadfast love and faithfulness.
- `general-simple-guided-prayer`: A gentle, ecumenical prayer session: pause, confess, give thanks, listen, intercede, and close with the Lord's Prayer.
- `go-make-disciples`: A guided prayer on Matthew 28:18-20 and Luke 15:7 — an encouraging call to the Great Commission, the joy of sharing faith, and courage to step into the world with Christ.
- `grateful-heart`: A guided prayer to cultivate gratitude for God's daily gifts, rooted in Psalm 107:1, 1 Thessalonians 5:18, and James 1:17.
- `hope-in-hard-times`: A guided prayer of encouragement and comfort for anyone walking through difficulty, rooted in Psalm 46:1-3, Isaiah 41:10, and 2 Corinthians 1:3-4.
- `jewish-guided-prayer`: Shema, blessings, reflection on the day, and personal supplication.
- `love-and-forgive-our-enemies`: A guided prayer for the hard work of loving and forgiving enemies, grounded in Matthew 5:44 — pray for those who persecute you — and the freedom that forgiveness brings.
- `love-god-and-neighbor`: A guided prayer on the two greatest commandments — love God with all your heart, soul, mind, and strength, and love your neighbor as yourself.
- `orthodox-guided-prayer`: Trisagion, Jesus Prayer, stillness before icons in the heart, intercession, and blessing.
- `peace-and-rest`: A guided session to release anxiety, receive God's peace, and rest in stillness before sleep or a stressful moment.
- `pray-for-leaders`: A guided prayer for leaders in every sphere of life, grounded in 1 Timothy 2:1-2 — asking for peace, dignity, and wisdom for those in authority.
- `pray-for-the-peace-of-jerusalem`: A guided prayer for Jerusalem and all its people, rooted in Psalm 122:6 — asking for peace, security, and flourishing for the city sacred to three faiths.
- `pray-for-the-persecuted-church`: A guided prayer for Christians facing persecution around the world, grounded in Hebrews 13:3 and Matthew 5:44, with promises of courage from Isaiah 41:10 and Romans 8:38-39.
- `protestant-guided-prayer`: Scripture-centered session with confession, thanksgiving, listening, intercession, the Lord's Prayer, and a doxology.

## Format

Guided prayer packages use the `guided` package type. Each `.prayer.zip` contains a single JSON file with a guided session payload: prompts, durations, and optional TTS/audio settings.
